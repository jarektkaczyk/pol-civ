# Economy site sources: Made in Poland, Tech and games

## Conclusions

- 2 sources feed `economy.json` and two Map switches: "Made in Poland" (33 curated places) and "Tech and games" (32 curated tech landmarks plus 85 Wikidata game studios in 18 towns). Total: 150 items.
- Every curated fact was re-read at its source on 2026-11-26 and checked again by an independent fact-check the same day (11 facts reworded or dropped). Each has a `ref` URL and the `year` of its figure. All `ref` pages but two are cached through `Source.download()`: lg.com refuses automated reads (read by hand), and forbes.com (Brainly) refuses all automated reads, so that figure was read from the Wikipedia infobox that cites it (`ref2`).
- Gmina codes come from point in polygon against the LAU 2024 outlines. All 150 points fall inside a gmina outline.
- Game studios come from Wikidata (CC0). The best-known game of the 30 studios with the most sitelinks was checked by hand: 3 picks changed (CD Projekt Red now shows The Witcher 3), 3 studios dropped (CD Projekt S.A., Nibris, Reality Pump), 2 moved to their real town (Techland to Wrocław by its KRS seat, Tate Multimedia to Warszawa).

## Critical learnings

- Wikidata Query Service limited anonymous clients to 1 request per minute on 2026-11-26 (an outage rule). `pl_tech_sites` waits 90 s and retries on HTTP 429, up to 10 times.
- "Largest in Europe" is mostly a company claim. The page shows a record only when an independent page states it, or words it as the company's claim ("says", "calls itself").
- Wikidata headquarters can be stale and are not always the legal seat: it puts Techland in Ostrów Wielkopolski, but its KRS seat is Wrocław (ul. Jana Szczyrki 11), and Tate Multimedia in Kraków, but it is in Warszawa. `studio_place` in `pl_tech_sites.json` fixes them with the page that shows the town. Curated tech items with a seat abroad (Huuuge: Dover) carry `office: true` and the card says "office".
- Wikidata has no reliable activity signal for studios: game lists are incomplete (PlayWay's newest listed game is from 2015), and closed studios often lack a dissolution date (Reality Pump closed in 2015). Closed studios go in `studio_drop` with the page that shows it.
- Dates inside a fact are written in English ("April 2026"); `write_web()` turns them into ISO and the browser formats them per language. The Polish catalogue key is the ISO form (a unit test checks every fact).
- gov.pl, stat.gov.pl and pesa.pl need `SSL_CERT_FILE` with the macOS system roots, as for BDL.

## Datasets looked for and not used

- Polish Investment Zone (PSI) decisions by company and gmina: the ministry publishes totals only (646 decisions, PLN 22 bn in 2025). The UOKiK SUDOP API has aid by beneficiary but publishes sole traders' data, so only aggregates could be shown.
- Startup Poland "Polskie Startupy", ABSL sector reports, PFR Ventures portfolio, Dealroom: sold, behind a form, blocked (HTTP 403) or under all-rights-reserved terms.
- GPW company cards and market caps: no reuse licence; not used.
- Wikidata for scale-ups: most Polish scale-ups have no item with a Polish HQ (Docplanner, Packhelp, Nomagic and others), so the landmarks are curated by hand.
- Silent Eight: no fetchable page states a fact; dropped.
- Packhelp: the only dated figures are from 2021; packhelp.com refuses automated reads and its current figures are undated; dropped.
- LG Energy Solution capacity in GWh: sources disagree (70, 80, 86, 90 GWh); the page shows no capacity.
- Mushrooms and candles (national records): no verified place to anchor them; left out.
