# Society sources (W4)

## Conclusions

- 9 sources feed the society lenses, the wonders, the military layer and the Culture, Knowledge and Military advisors. Each one processes from cache with `--offline`.
- Gmina codes come from PKW 6-digit codes plus the type digit from the GUS BDL unit list (level 6). Poland has 2,479 gminas since 1 Jan 2025 (Szczawa 1207132 and Grabówka 2002162 are new).
- Broadband by gmina is not available as a machine-readable file. Life expectancy is left to the stats worker (W2).

## Critical learnings

- `bdl.stat.gov.pl` sends a certificate chain to a root (Certum Trusted Root CA) that is missing from `/etc/ssl/cert.pem` on this Mac. Run fetches with `SSL_CERT_FILE` pointing to a bundle that adds that root. The BDL anonymous limit is shared by all workers on one IP; `pl_soc_common.bdl_download` waits for `Retry-After` on HTTP 429.
- The BDL unit list keeps old units: a gmina that changed type is listed twice. The urban-rural code (type 3) is the current one.
- PKW 2025 files have a timestamp in their names. It is read from the site's `config.blob` (protobuf).
- whc.unesco.org, wojsko-polskie.pl, GDOŚ WFS, internet.gov.pl and zabytek.pl refuse automated reads. The sources use data.unesco.org, dane.gov.pl, gov.pl, NATO PDFs and cited Wikipedia pages instead.

## Datasets looked for and not used

- Broadband (fixed 100 Mb/s or more by gmina): UKE stopped the coverage report and SIIS was shut down (dane.gov.pl dataset 588). internet.gov.pl and the 2025 SIDUSIS data are an interactive map (403 to scripts) and PDFs per voivodeship.
- Life expectancy by powiat: left to W2 (stats), as `docs/BLUEPRINT.md` section 7 shares it. BDL has no doctors-per-10,000 variable by powiat, so the rate is computed.
- GIOŚ modelled annual means per gmina or powiat: GIOŚ publishes only a grid in an ArcGIS map service; sampling it per gmina needs about 7,500 requests.
- National park outlines: the GDOŚ WFS (`sdi.gdos.gov.pl`) refuses automated reads (Incapsula 403); the static shapefile is from 2021.
- 1997 and 2010 flood gmina lists: scanned PDFs only (1997 in the 49 pre-1999 voivodeships). The 2024 list of gminas eligible for flood aid (Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1371, consolidated Dz.U. 2026 poz. 948) is a PDF only.
- ISOK / Wody Polskie flood-hazard share per gmina: only WFS polygons (GML); the share needs a polygon overlay.
- ISKK 2025 count: not published yet (expected mid-December 2026).
- Official garrison counts by voivodeship: not published; the MON garrison regulation (Dz.U. 2022 poz. 1868) has no counts.
- Wojsko Polskie unit pages (wojsko-polskie.pl), zabytek.pl, whc.unesco.org: refuse automated reads; replaced as listed above.
