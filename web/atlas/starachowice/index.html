Starachowice

Starachowice, powiat starachowicki, Świętokrzyskie

Starachowice has the 4th oldest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 49.6 years.

Starachowice is a town in powiat starachowicki, Świętokrzyskie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.3% of valid votes in Starachowice and Karol Nawrocki 50.7%. Turnout was 67.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
