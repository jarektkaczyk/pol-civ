# Sieradz

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Sieradz had 37,514 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 11.9% of its residents.

Sieradz is a town in powiat sieradzki, Łódzkie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.4% of valid votes in Sieradz and Karol Nawrocki 48.5%. Turnout was 73.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
