## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 37.7% of valid votes in the gmina of Wyszków and Karol Nawrocki 62.3%. Turnout was 74.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
