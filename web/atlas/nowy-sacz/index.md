# Nowy Sącz

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/nowy-sacz/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Nowy Sącz has the 9th most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 20.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Nowy Sącz is a city with powiat rights in Małopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 39.6% of valid votes in Nowy Sącz and Karol Nawrocki 60.4%. Turnout was 73.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 20.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Nowy Sącz, ul. Nadbrzeżna (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
