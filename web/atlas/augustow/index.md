# Augustów

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/augustow/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Augustów had 28,425 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.2% of its residents.

Augustów is a town in powiat augustowski, Podlaskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 43.0% of valid votes in Augustów and Karol Nawrocki 57.0%. Turnout was 66.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
