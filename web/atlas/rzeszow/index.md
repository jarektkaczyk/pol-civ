# Rzeszów

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/rzeszow/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/rzeszow/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Rzeszów has 177 university students per 1,000 residents, the 5th highest share of the 84 towns of 20,000+ with a university.

Rzeszów is a city with powiat rights in Podkarpackie voivodeship. It is the capital of Podkarpackie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 48.3% of valid votes in Rzeszów and Karol Nawrocki 51.7%. Turnout was 75.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Rzeszów, ul. Rejtana (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
