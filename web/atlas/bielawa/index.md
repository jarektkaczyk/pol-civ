# Bielawa

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/bielawa/

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Bielawa expects 3,196 zł per resident from its share of personal income tax in 2026, the lowest of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Bielawa is a town in powiat dzierżoniowski, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.7 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat dzierżoniowski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 7,916 workers or more, 1.7 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 53.3% of valid votes in Bielawa and Karol Nawrocki 46.7%. Turnout was 63.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
