The average gross wage in Gdańsk was 10,743 zł a month in May 2026, the 4th highest for any of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Gdańsk is a city with powiat rights in Pomorskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Pomorskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 70.0% of valid votes in Gdańsk and Karol Nawrocki 30.0%. Turnout was 79.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 16.1 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Gdańsk, ul. Powstańców Warszawskich (0 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
