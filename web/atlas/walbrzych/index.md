# Wałbrzych

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/walbrzych/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/walbrzych/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Wałbrzych lost 15.5% of its residents between 2015 and 2025, the 2nd steepest fall of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Wałbrzych is a city with powiat rights in Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.8 times the national share of workers.

Jobs by employer seat on 31 December 2025, against the share of each kind of work in Poland.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 10,662 workers, 1.8 times the national share
- Aqueduct & power district (Power & heat, Water & waste): 1,262 workers, 2.1 times the national share
- Theater square district (Arts, sport & recreation): 552 workers, 1.5 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 61.8% of valid votes in Wałbrzych and Karol Nawrocki 38.2%. Turnout was 65.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Wałbrzych, ul. Wysockiego (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Wałbrzych belongs to the Wałbrzych metro area, 148,448 residents in 9 gminas.
