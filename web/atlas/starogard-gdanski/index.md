# Starogard Gdański

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/starogard-gdanski/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Starogard Gdański had 44,672 residents on 31 December 2025, 7.5% fewer than in 2015.

Starogard Gdański is a town in powiat starogardzki, Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.5% of valid votes in Starogard Gdański and Karol Nawrocki 48.5%. Turnout was 66.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
