Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/zambrow/

Turnout in Zambrów was 64.5% in the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, the 7th lowest of the 204 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Zambrów is a town in powiat zambrowski, Podlaskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 34.3% of valid votes in Zambrów and Karol Nawrocki 65.7%. Turnout was 64.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
