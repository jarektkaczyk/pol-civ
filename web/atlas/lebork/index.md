# Lębork

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/lebork/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/lebork/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Lębork had 33,538 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 5.0% of its residents.

Lębork is a town in powiat lęborski, Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 59.6% of valid votes in Lębork and Karol Nawrocki 40.4%. Turnout was 70.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
