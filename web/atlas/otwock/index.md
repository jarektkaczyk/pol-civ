# Otwock

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/otwock/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Otwock had 43,118 residents on 31 December 2025, 4.2% fewer than in 2015.

Otwock is a town in powiat otwocki, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.7% of valid votes in Otwock and Karol Nawrocki 48.3%. Turnout was 78.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 19.3 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Otwock, ul. Brzozowa (4 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
