# Bydgoszcz

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/bydgoszcz/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Bydgoszcz is the 9th largest city in Poland, with 319,528 residents on 30 June 2026.

Bydgoszcz is a city with powiat rights in Kujawsko-Pomorskie voivodeship. It is the seat of the voivode of Kujawsko-Pomorskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 64.4% of valid votes in Bydgoszcz and Karol Nawrocki 35.6%. Turnout was 73.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 17.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Bydgoszcz, ul. Warszawska (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
