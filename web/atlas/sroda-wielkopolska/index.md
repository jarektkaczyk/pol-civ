## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.0% of valid votes in the gmina of Środa Wielkopolska and Karol Nawrocki 43.0%. Turnout was 71.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
