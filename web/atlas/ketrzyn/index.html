2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.8% of valid votes in Kętrzyn and Karol Nawrocki 42.2%. Turnout was 64.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
