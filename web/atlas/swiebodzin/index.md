## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.3% of valid votes in the gmina of Świebodzin and Karol Nawrocki 43.7%. Turnout was 66.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
