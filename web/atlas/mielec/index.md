# Mielec

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/mielec/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/mielec/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Mielec had 55,847 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 7.6% of its residents.

Mielec is a town in powiat mielecki, Podkarpackie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 39.5% of valid votes in Mielec and Karol Nawrocki 60.5%. Turnout was 70.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Mielec, ul. Biernackiego (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
