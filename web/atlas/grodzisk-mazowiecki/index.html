Grodzisk Mazowiecki

Grodzisk Mazowiecki, powiat grodziski, Mazowieckie

The gmina of Grodzisk Mazowiecki has the 6th youngest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 41.2 years.

Grodzisk Mazowiecki is a town in powiat grodziski, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.8% of valid votes in the gmina of Grodzisk Mazowiecki and Karol Nawrocki 42.2%. Turnout was 79.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 20.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Żyrardów, ul. Roosevelta (14 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
