# Oława

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/olawa/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Oława had 32,872 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it grew by 1.1%.

Oława is a town in powiat oławski, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.1% of valid votes in Oława and Karol Nawrocki 43.9%. Turnout was 72.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 12.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Brzeg, ul. Poprzeczna (16 km).
