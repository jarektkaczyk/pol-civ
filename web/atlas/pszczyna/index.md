## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 44.8% of valid votes in the gmina of Pszczyna and Karol Nawrocki 55.2%. Turnout was 74.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Goczałkowice Zdrój, ul. Parkowa (7 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
