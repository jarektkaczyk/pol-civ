2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 69.1% of valid votes in Luboń and Karol Nawrocki 30.9%. Turnout was 78.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
