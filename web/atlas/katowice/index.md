# Katowice

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/katowice/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Katowice has 187 university students per 1,000 residents, the 4th highest share of the 84 towns of 20,000+ with a university.

Katowice is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Śląskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 63.1% of valid votes in Katowice and Karol Nawrocki 36.9%. Turnout was 74.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.1 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Katowice, ul. Dudy-Gracza (6 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby
