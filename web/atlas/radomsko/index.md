## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 48.3% of valid votes in Radomsko and Karol Nawrocki 51.7%. Turnout was 70.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
