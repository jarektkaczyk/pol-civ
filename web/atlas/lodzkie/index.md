# Łódzkie

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/lodzkie/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/lodzkie/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Women in Łódzkie have the shortest life expectancy of the 16 voivodeships: 81.4 years at birth in 2025.

Its capital is Łódź.

It has 60 towns; 13 of them have 20,000 or more residents.

## Heritage, parks and landmarks

- Uniwersytet Łódzki: University, 23,985 students on 31 December 2025
- Elektrownia Bełchatów: Largest operating power station: 5,097 MW (2025)

## Energy and resources

- Elektrownia Bełchatów: Lignite power plant, 5,097 MW
- Elektrociepłownia EC3: Hard-coal power plant, 205 MW
- Elektrociepłownia EC4: Hard-coal power plant, 198 MW
- KWB Bełchatów (fields Bełchatów and Szczerców): Lignite mine, 31.1 Mt lignite (2025)
