Kościerzyna has the 3rd cleanest air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 10.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Kościerzyna is a town in powiat kościerski, Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.2% of valid votes in Kościerzyna and Karol Nawrocki 43.8%. Turnout was 70.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 10.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kościerzyna, ul. Targowa (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
