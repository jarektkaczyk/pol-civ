# Żory

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/zory/

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Żory had 61,804 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 0.1% of its residents.

Żory is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 47.2% of valid votes in Żory and Karol Nawrocki 52.8%. Turnout was 71.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Żory, Os. Gen. Władysława Sikorskiego (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
