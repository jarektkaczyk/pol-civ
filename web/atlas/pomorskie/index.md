# Pomorskie

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/pomorskie/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/pomorskie/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Pomorskie grew by 2.2% between 2015 and 2025, the 2nd fastest growth of the 16 voivodeships.

Its capital is Gdańsk.

It has 43 towns; 15 of them have 20,000 or more residents.

## Heritage, parks and landmarks

- Castle of the Teutonic Order in Malbork: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1997
- Gdynia Modernist City Centre: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2026
- Port of Gdańsk: Busiest seaport by cargo tonnage: 75,324 thousand tonnes (2025)

## Energy and resources

- Lubiatowo-Kopalino nuclear power plant: Nuclear power plant, 3,750 MW (planned)
- Baltica 2 offshore wind farm: Offshore wind farm, 1,500 MW (under_construction)
- Baltic Power offshore wind farm: Offshore wind farm, 1,200 MW (under_construction)
- Bałtyk 2 offshore wind farm: Offshore wind farm, 720 MW (under_construction)
- Bałtyk 3 offshore wind farm: Offshore wind farm, 720 MW (under_construction)
- Elektrownia Wodna Żarnowiec: Pumped-storage plant, 716 MW
- Gdańsk refinery: Oil refinery
