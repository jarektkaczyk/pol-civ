Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/skarzysko-kamienna/

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.8% of valid votes in Skarżysko-Kamienna and Karol Nawrocki 50.2%. Turnout was 68.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality
