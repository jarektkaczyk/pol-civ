# Głogów

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/glogow/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/glogow/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Głogów had 60,287 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 11.7% of its residents.

Głogów is a town in powiat głogowski, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Copper town. Głogów Głęboki-Przemysłowy (shaft GG-1).

These figures describe the jobs in powiat głogowski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Theater square district (Arts, sport & recreation): 298 workers, 1.3 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.8% of valid votes in Głogów and Karol Nawrocki 48.1%. Turnout was 69.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 15.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Wschowa ul. Kazimierza Wielkiego (23 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
