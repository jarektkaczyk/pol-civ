# Brzeg

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/brzeg/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Brzeg had 32,572 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 10.3% of its residents.

Brzeg is a town in powiat brzeski, Opolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 52.3% of valid votes in Brzeg and Karol Nawrocki 47.7%. Turnout was 68.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality
