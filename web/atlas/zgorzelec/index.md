# Zgorzelec

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/zgorzelec/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/zgorzelec/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Zgorzelec had 27,941 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 10.4% of its residents.

Zgorzelec is a town in powiat zgorzelecki, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.5% of valid votes in Zgorzelec and Karol Nawrocki 42.5%. Turnout was 69.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
