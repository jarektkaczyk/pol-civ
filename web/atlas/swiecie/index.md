## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.6 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat świecki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 8,997 workers, 1.6 times the national share
- Aqueduct & power district (Power & heat, Water & waste): 710 workers, 1.3 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 58.3% of valid votes in the gmina of Świecie and Karol Nawrocki 41.7%. Turnout was 66.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.0 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Świecie, al. Jana Pawła II (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
