# Dolnośląskie

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/dolnoslaskie/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Dolnośląskie has the 2nd highest average gross wage of the 16 voivodeships: 9,208 zł a month in May 2026.

Its capital is Wrocław.

It has 93 towns; 16 of them have 20,000 or more residents.

## Heritage, parks and landmarks

- Churches of Peace in Jawor and Świdnica: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2001
- Centennial Hall in Wrocław: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2006
- Karkonoski Park Narodowy: National park, 2,400,000 visits in 2024
- Park Narodowy Gór Stołowych: National park, 1,296,300 visits in 2024
- Uniwersytet Wrocławski: University, 21,426 students on 31 December 2025
