Bełchatów has the 5th most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 21.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Bełchatów is a town in powiat bełchatowski, Łódzkie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Coal town. KWB Bełchatów (fields Bełchatów and Szczerców).

These figures describe the jobs in powiat bełchatowski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 11,655 workers, 1.6 times the national share; Mining alone 12.5 times
- Aqueduct & power district (Power & heat, Water & waste): 4,577 workers, 6.4 times the national share; Power & heat alone 11.4 times

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 37.5% of valid votes in Bełchatów and Karol Nawrocki 62.5%. Turnout was 72.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 21.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Piotrków Trybunalski, ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście (23 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
