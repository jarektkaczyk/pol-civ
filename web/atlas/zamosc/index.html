Zamość

Zamość, Lubelskie

Zamość had 56,521 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 12.3% of its residents.

Zamość is a city with powiat rights in Lubelskie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 43.1% of valid votes in Zamość and Karol Nawrocki 56.9%. Turnout was 68.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
