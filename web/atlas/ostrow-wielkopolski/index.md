# Ostrów Wielkopolski

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/ostrow-wielkopolski/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Ostrów Wielkopolski had 67,850 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.2% of its residents.

Ostrów Wielkopolski is a town in powiat ostrowski, Wielkopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 61.2% of valid votes in Ostrów Wielkopolski and Karol Nawrocki 38.8%. Turnout was 72.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 19.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kalisz, ul. Wyszyńskiego (20 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
