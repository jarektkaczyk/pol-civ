# Myszków

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/myszkow/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/myszkow/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Myszków had 28,493 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 11.3% of its residents.

Myszków is a town in powiat myszkowski, Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 47.2% of valid votes in Myszków and Karol Nawrocki 52.8%. Turnout was 68.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
