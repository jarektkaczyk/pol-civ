# Łomża

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/lomza/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/lomza/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Łomża had 58,706 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.1% of its residents.

Łomża is a city with powiat rights in Podlaskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 38.3% of valid votes in Łomża and Karol Nawrocki 61.7%. Turnout was 69.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 17.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Łomża, ul. Sikorskiego (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
