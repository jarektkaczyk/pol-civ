## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 42.1% of valid votes in the gmina of Polkowice and Karol Nawrocki 57.9%. Turnout was 69.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
