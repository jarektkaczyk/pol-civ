# Żywiec

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Żywiec has the 2nd most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 25.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Żywiec is a town in powiat żywiecki, Śląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.6 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat żywiecki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 12,439 workers, 1.6 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 1,436 workers, 1.6 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 47.9% of valid votes in Żywiec and Karol Nawrocki 52.1%. Turnout was 71.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 25.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Żywiec, ul. Kopernika (5 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
