# Jelenia Góra

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/jelenia-gora/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Jelenia Góra has the 10th oldest population of the 204 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 48.6 years.

Jelenia Góra is a city with powiat rights in Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 64.7% of valid votes in Jelenia Góra and Karol Nawrocki 35.3%. Turnout was 70.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 16.1 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Jelenia Góra, ul. Ogińskiego (7 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
