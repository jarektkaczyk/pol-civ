## City type and districts

City type: Coal town. KWK Silesia.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat bielski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 18,447 workers, 1.9 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 1,416 workers, 1.3 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 50.7% of valid votes in the gmina of Czechowice-Dziedzice and Karol Nawrocki 49.3%. Turnout was 74.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Goczałkowice Zdrój, ul. Parkowa (4 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Czechowice-Dziedzice belongs to the Bielsko-Biała metro area, 360,759 residents in 14 gminas. Its core town is Bielsko-Biała.
