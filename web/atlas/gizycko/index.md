# Giżycko

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/gizycko/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/gizycko/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Giżycko had 26,295 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 10.9% of its residents.

Giżycko is a town in powiat giżycki, Warmińsko-Mazurskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Garrison town. 15 BZ.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat giżycki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Government plaza district (Public administration & defence): 10,356 workers, 6.3 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 724 workers, 1.4 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 61.4% of valid votes in Giżycko and Karol Nawrocki 38.6%. Turnout was 66.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 13.0 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Giżycko, ul. Wodociągowa (5 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
