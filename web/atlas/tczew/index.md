# Tczew

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/tczew/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/tczew/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Tczew had 55,678 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.9% of its residents.

Tczew is a town in powiat tczewski, Pomorskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 2.1 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat tczewski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 13,540 workers or more, 2.1 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 1,086 workers, 1.4 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.4% of valid votes in Tczew and Karol Nawrocki 43.6%. Turnout was 70.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
