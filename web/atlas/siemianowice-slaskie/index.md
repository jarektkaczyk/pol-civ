# Siemianowice Śląskie

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/siemianowice-slaskie/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Siemianowice Śląskie had 62,311 residents on 31 December 2025, 8.7% fewer than in 2015.

Siemianowice Śląskie is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 60.0% of valid votes in Siemianowice Śląskie and Karol Nawrocki 40.0%. Turnout was 69.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.1 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Katowice, ul. Dudy-Gracza (6 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Siemianowice Śląskie belongs to the Górnośląsko-Zagłębiowska metro area (PL010F, PL055F, PL061F, PL070F), 2,021,558 residents in 39 gminas. Its core town is Katowice.

- Tarnowskie Góry Lead-Silver-Zinc Mine and its Underground Water Management System: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2017, 20 km away
