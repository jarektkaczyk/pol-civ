# Przemyśl

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/przemysl/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Przemyśl had 54,352 residents on 31 December 2025, 13.3% fewer than in 2015.

Przemyśl is a city with powiat rights in Podkarpackie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 45.7% of valid votes in Przemyśl and Karol Nawrocki 54.3%. Turnout was 66.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
