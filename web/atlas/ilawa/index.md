# Iława

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/ilawa/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Iława had 31,798 residents on 31 December 2025, 4.2% fewer than in 2015.

Iława is a town in powiat iławski, Warmińsko-Mazurskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.6% of valid votes in Iława and Karol Nawrocki 42.4%. Turnout was 70.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
