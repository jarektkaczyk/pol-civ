## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 67.5% of valid votes in the gmina of Swarzędz and Karol Nawrocki 32.5%. Turnout was 78.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Poznań ul. Szwajcarska (9 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
