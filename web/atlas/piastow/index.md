## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 55.3% of valid votes in Piastów and Karol Nawrocki 44.7%. Turnout was 81.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
