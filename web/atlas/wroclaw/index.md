# Wrocław

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/wroclaw/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/wroclaw/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Wrocław has 196 university students per 1,000 residents, the 2nd highest share of the 84 towns of 20,000+ with a university.

Wrocław is a city with powiat rights in Dolnośląskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Dolnośląskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 67.1% of valid votes in Wrocław and Karol Nawrocki 32.9%. Turnout was 78.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 15.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Wrocław, wyb. Conrada-Korzeniowskiego (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
