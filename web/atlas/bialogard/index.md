# Białogard

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/bialogard/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Registered unemployment around Białogard (powiat białogardzki) was 17.1% in October 2026, the 2nd highest for any of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Białogard is a town in powiat białogardzki, Zachodniopomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 59.1% of valid votes in Białogard and Karol Nawrocki 40.9%. Turnout was 65.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
