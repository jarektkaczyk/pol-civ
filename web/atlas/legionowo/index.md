# Legionowo

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/legionowo/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/legionowo/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Legionowo had 51,732 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 4.2% of its residents.

Legionowo is a town in powiat legionowski, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.5% of valid votes in Legionowo and Karol Nawrocki 42.5%. Turnout was 79.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 13.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Legionowo, ul. Zegrzyńska (5 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
