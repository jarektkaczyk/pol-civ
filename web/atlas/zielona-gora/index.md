# Zielona Góra

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/zielona-gora/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/zielona-gora/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Zielona Góra has the 9th cleanest air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 11.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Zielona Góra is a city with powiat rights in Lubuskie voivodeship. It is the seat of the regional assembly (sejmik) of Lubuskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 68.1% of valid votes in Zielona Góra and Karol Nawrocki 31.9%. Turnout was 74.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 11.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Zielona Góra ul. Krótka (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
