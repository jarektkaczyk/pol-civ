# Mikołów

Mikołów had 41,487 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it grew by 3.9%.

Mikołów is a town in powiat mikołowski, Śląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Coal town. KWK Bolesław Śmiały.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat mikołowski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 6,905 workers, 1.3 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 58.1% of valid votes in Mikołów and Karol Nawrocki 41.9%. Turnout was 74.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.3 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Katowice, ul. Kossutha (12 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Mikołów belongs to the Górnośląsko-Zagłębiowska metro area (PL010F, PL055F, PL061F, PL070F), 2,013,732 residents in 39 gminas. Its core town is Katowice.
