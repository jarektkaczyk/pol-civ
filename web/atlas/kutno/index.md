# Kutno

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/kutno/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Kutno has the 8th most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 20.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Kutno is a town in powiat kutnowski, Łódzkie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.5 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat kutnowski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 8,025 workers or more, 1.5 times the national share
- Aqueduct & power district (Power & heat, Water & waste): 1,084 workers, 2.1 times the national share; Water & waste alone 3.2 times
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 1,103 workers, 1.8 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.3% of valid votes in Kutno and Karol Nawrocki 50.7%. Turnout was 71.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 20.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kutno, ul. 1 Maja (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
