# Olsztyn

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/olsztyn/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/olsztyn/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Olsztyn is the 20th largest city in Poland, with 164,999 residents on 30 June 2026.

Olsztyn is a city with powiat rights in Warmińsko-Mazurskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Warmińsko-Mazurskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 63.5% of valid votes in Olsztyn and Karol Nawrocki 36.4%. Turnout was 74.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 12.2 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Olsztyn, ul. Puszkina (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
