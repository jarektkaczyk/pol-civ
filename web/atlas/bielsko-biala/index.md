Bielsko-Biała has the 4th most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 22.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Bielsko-Biała is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 55.9% of valid votes in Bielsko-Biała and Karol Nawrocki 44.1%. Turnout was 74.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 22.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Bielsko-Biała, ul.Partyzantów (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Bielsko-Biała belongs to the Bielsko-Biała metro area, 360,759 residents in 14 gminas.

- Auschwitz Birkenau German Nazi Concentration and Extermination Camp (1940-1945): Memorial and museum, UNESCO World Heritage since 1979, 25 km away
