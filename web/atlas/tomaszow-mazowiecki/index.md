# Tomaszów Mazowiecki

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/tomaszow-mazowiecki/

Last updated: 2026-11-27

Tomaszów Mazowiecki had 55,696 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 12.4% of its residents.

Tomaszów Mazowiecki is a town in powiat tomaszowski, Łódzkie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 48.5% of valid votes in Tomaszów Mazowiecki and Karol Nawrocki 51.5%. Turnout was 71.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
