Zawiercie

Zawiercie, powiat zawierciański, Śląskie

Zawiercie had 44,922 residents on 31 December 2025, 11.3% fewer than in 2015.

Zawiercie is a town in powiat zawierciański, Śląskie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 52.2% of valid votes in Zawiercie and Karol Nawrocki 47.8%. Turnout was 68.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
