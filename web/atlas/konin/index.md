# Konin

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/konin/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Konin had 64,756 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 14.0% of its residents.

Konin is a city with powiat rights in Wielkopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 52.6% of valid votes in Konin and Karol Nawrocki 47.4%. Turnout was 71.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
