# Tarnowskie Góry

Last updated: 2026-11-28

Tarnowskie Góry had 60,876 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 0.1% of its residents.

Tarnowskie Góry is a town in powiat tarnogórski, Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.3% of valid votes in Tarnowskie Góry and Karol Nawrocki 42.7%. Turnout was 71.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Tarnowskie Góry, ul. Litewska (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
