# Sanok

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/sanok/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/sanok/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Sanok had 33,328 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 13.0% of its residents.

Sanok is a town in powiat sanocki, Podkarpackie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 45.4% of valid votes in Sanok and Karol Nawrocki 54.6%. Turnout was 66.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
