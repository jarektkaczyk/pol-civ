Registered unemployment around Bartoszyce (powiat bartoszycki) was 17.5% in October 2026, the highest for any of the 204 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Bartoszyce is a town in powiat bartoszycki, Warmińsko-Mazurskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.1% of valid votes in Bartoszyce and Karol Nawrocki 43.9%. Turnout was 65.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
