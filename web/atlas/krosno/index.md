# Krosno

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/krosno/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/krosno/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Krosno had 43,136 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 7.5% of its residents.

Krosno is a city with powiat rights in Podkarpackie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 44.4% of valid votes in Krosno and Karol Nawrocki 55.6%. Turnout was 70.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 12.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Krosno, ul. Kletówki (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
