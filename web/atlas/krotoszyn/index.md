## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 47.8% of valid votes in the gmina of Krotoszyn and Karol Nawrocki 52.2%. Turnout was 68.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 26.3 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Krotoszyn, ul. Olimpijska (6 km).
