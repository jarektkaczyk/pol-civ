# Mińsk Mazowiecki

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/minsk-mazowiecki/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/minsk-mazowiecki/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Mińsk Mazowiecki had 39,352 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 2.1% of its residents.

Mińsk Mazowiecki is a town in powiat miński, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 50.3% of valid votes in Mińsk Mazowiecki and Karol Nawrocki 49.7%. Turnout was 79.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
