# Kraśnik

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/krasnik/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Kraśnik expects 3,235 zł per resident from its share of personal income tax in 2026, the 2nd lowest of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Kraśnik is a town in powiat kraśnicki, Lubelskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 39.6% of valid votes in Kraśnik and Karol Nawrocki 60.4%. Turnout was 67.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
