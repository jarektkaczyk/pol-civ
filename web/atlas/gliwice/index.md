Gliwice is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 60.9% of valid votes in Gliwice and Karol Nawrocki 39.1%. Turnout was 73.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 19.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Gliwice, ul. Mewy (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Gliwice belongs to the Górnośląsko-Zagłębiowska metro area (PL010F, PL055F, PL061F, PL070F), 2,013,732 residents in 39 gminas. Its core town is Katowice.

- Tarnowskie Góry Lead-Silver-Zinc Mine and its Underground Water Management System: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2017, 21 km away
