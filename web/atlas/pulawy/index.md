Puławy has the 6th oldest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 49.3 years.

Puławy is a town in powiat puławski, Lubelskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 47.9% of valid votes in Puławy and Karol Nawrocki 52.1%. Turnout was 72.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
