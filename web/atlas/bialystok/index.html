Białystok

Białystok, Podlaskie

Białystok is the 10th largest city in Poland, with 288,476 residents on 30 June 2026.

Białystok is a city with powiat rights in Podlaskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Podlaskie.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.0% of valid votes in Białystok and Karol Nawrocki 49.0%. Turnout was 73.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
