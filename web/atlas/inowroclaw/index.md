## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 62.2% of valid votes in Inowrocław and Karol Nawrocki 37.8%. Turnout was 68.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
