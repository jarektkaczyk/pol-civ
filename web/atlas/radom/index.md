# Radom

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/radom/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Radom is the 16th largest town in Poland, with 191,996 residents on 31 December 2025.

Radom is a city with powiat rights in Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 44.6% of valid votes in Radom and Karol Nawrocki 55.4%. Turnout was 73.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
