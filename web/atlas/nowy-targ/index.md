Nowy Targ expects 3,406 zł per resident from its share of personal income tax in 2026, the 6th lowest of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Nowy Targ is a town in powiat nowotarski, Małopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 43.5% of valid votes in Nowy Targ and Karol Nawrocki 56.5%. Turnout was 67.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Zakopane, ul. Sienkiewicza (22 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
