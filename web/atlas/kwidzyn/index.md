# Kwidzyn

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Kwidzyn had 36,007 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.4% of its residents.

Kwidzyn is a town in powiat kwidzyński, Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 60.2% of valid votes in Kwidzyn and Karol Nawrocki 39.8%. Turnout was 68.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
