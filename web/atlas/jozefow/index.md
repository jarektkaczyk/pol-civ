Józefów is a town in powiat otwocki, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 12,780 workers, 1.5 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 58.1% of valid votes in Józefów and Karol Nawrocki 41.9%. Turnout was 83.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 19.3 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Otwock, ul. Brzozowa (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
