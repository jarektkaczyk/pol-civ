# Włocławek

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/wloclawek/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/wloclawek/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Włocławek had 97,435 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 13.3% of its residents.

Włocławek is a city with powiat rights in Kujawsko-Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 60.5% of valid votes in Włocławek and Karol Nawrocki 39.5%. Turnout was 67.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Włocławek, ul. Okrzei (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
