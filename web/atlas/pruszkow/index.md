## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 59.9% of valid votes in Pruszków and Karol Nawrocki 40.1%. Turnout was 80.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Piastów, ul. Pułaskiego (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
