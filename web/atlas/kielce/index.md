# Kielce

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/kielce/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/kielce/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Kielce is the 18th largest city in Poland, with 177,980 residents on 30 June 2026.

Kielce is a city with powiat rights in Świętokrzyskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Świętokrzyskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 53.7% of valid votes in Kielce and Karol Nawrocki 46.3%. Turnout was 74.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kielce, ul. Targowa (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
