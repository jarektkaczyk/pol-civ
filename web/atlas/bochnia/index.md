# Bochnia

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/bochnia/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/bochnia/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Bochnia had 28,126 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.4% of its residents.

Bochnia is a town in powiat bocheński, Małopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 45.5% of valid votes in Bochnia and Karol Nawrocki 54.5%. Turnout was 73.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.0 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Bochnia, ul. Konfederatów Barskich (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
