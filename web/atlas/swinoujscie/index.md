Świnoujście has the 2nd cleanest air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 9.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Świnoujście is a city with powiat rights in Zachodniopomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 64.0% of valid votes in Świnoujście and Karol Nawrocki 36.0%. Turnout was 72.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 9.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Świnoujście, ul. Białoruska (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
