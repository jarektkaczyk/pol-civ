# Malbork

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/malbork/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/malbork/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Malbork had 35,949 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 7.1% of its residents.

Malbork is a town in powiat malborski, Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 61.9% of valid votes in Malbork and Karol Nawrocki 38.1%. Turnout was 68.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
