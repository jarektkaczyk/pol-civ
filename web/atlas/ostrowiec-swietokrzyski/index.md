Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski has the oldest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 50.1 years.

Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski is a town in powiat ostrowiecki, Świętokrzyskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.1% of valid votes in Ostrowiec Świętokrzyski and Karol Nawrocki 50.9%. Turnout was 65.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
