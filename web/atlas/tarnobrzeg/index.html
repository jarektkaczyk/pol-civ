Tarnobrzeg

Tarnobrzeg, Podkarpackie

Tarnobrzeg had 42,488 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 10.6% of its residents.

Tarnobrzeg is a city with powiat rights in Podkarpackie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 48.4% of valid votes in Tarnobrzeg and Karol Nawrocki 51.6%. Turnout was 68.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
