# Świdnik

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/swidnik/

Świdnik had 36,215 residents on 31 December 2025, 9.6% fewer than in 2015.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 43.8% of valid votes in Świdnik and Karol Nawrocki 56.2%. Turnout was 72.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
