# Zduńska Wola

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/zdunska-wola/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

The average gross wage around Zduńska Wola (powiat zduńskowolski) was 7,317 zł a month in May 2026, the 10th lowest for any of the 204 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Zduńska Wola is a town in powiat zduńskowolski, Łódzkie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 48.4% of valid votes in Zduńska Wola and Karol Nawrocki 51.6%. Turnout was 71.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
