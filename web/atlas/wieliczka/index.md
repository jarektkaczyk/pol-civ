# Wieliczka

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/wieliczka/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/wieliczka/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

The gmina of Wieliczka has the 5th youngest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 41.0 years.

Wieliczka is a town in powiat wielicki, Małopolskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.7 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat wielicki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 15,888 workers or more, 1.7 times the national share
- Theater square district (Arts, sport & recreation): 1,003 workers, 1.7 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.2% of valid votes in the gmina of Wieliczka and Karol Nawrocki 50.8%. Turnout was 77.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kraków, ul. Bujaka (9 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Wieliczka belongs to the Kraków metro area, 1,468,599 residents in 40 gminas. Its core town is Kraków.

- Wieliczka and Bochnia Royal Salt Mines: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1978, in the town
- Historic Centre of Kraków: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1978, 12 km away
- Uniwersytet Jagielloński w Krakowie: University, 33,369 students on 31 December 2025, 13 km away
