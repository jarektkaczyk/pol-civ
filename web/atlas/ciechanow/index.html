Ciechanów

Ciechanów, powiat ciechanowski, Mazowieckie

Ciechanów had 41,109 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 7.3% of its residents.

Ciechanów is a town in powiat ciechanowski, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 46.1% of valid votes in Ciechanów and Karol Nawrocki 53.9%. Turnout was 72.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
