# Żagań

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/zagan/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Żagań had 22,648 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 13.1% of its residents.

Żagań is a town in powiat żagański, Lubuskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 62.0% of valid votes in Żagań and Karol Nawrocki 38.0%. Turnout was 66.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
