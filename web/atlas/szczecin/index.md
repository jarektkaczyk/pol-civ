# Szczecin

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/szczecin/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/szczecin/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Szczecin is the 7th largest city in Poland, with 382,271 residents on 30 June 2026.

Szczecin is a city with powiat rights in Zachodniopomorskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Zachodniopomorskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 64.2% of valid votes in Szczecin and Karol Nawrocki 35.8%. Turnout was 74.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 13.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Szczecin, ul. Piłsudskiego (4 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
