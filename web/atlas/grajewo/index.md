## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 35.2% of valid votes in Grajewo and Karol Nawrocki 64.8%. Turnout was 62.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
