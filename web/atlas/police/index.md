## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.3% of valid votes in the gmina of Police and Karol Nawrocki 42.7%. Turnout was 69.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
