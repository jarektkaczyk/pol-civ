## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 53.0% of valid votes in Żyrardów and Karol Nawrocki 47.0%. Turnout was 74.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 20.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Żyrardów, ul. Roosevelta (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
