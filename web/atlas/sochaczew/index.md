# Sochaczew

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/sochaczew/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Sochaczew has the 10th most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 20.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Sochaczew is a town in powiat sochaczewski, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.4% of valid votes in Sochaczew and Karol Nawrocki 50.6%. Turnout was 73.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
