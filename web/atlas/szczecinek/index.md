# Szczecinek

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/szczecinek/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Szczecinek has the cleanest air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 9.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Szczecinek is a town in powiat szczecinecki, Zachodniopomorskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

These figures describe the jobs in powiat szczecinecki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 4,320 workers, 1.4 times the national share
- Aqueduct & power district (Power & heat, Water & waste): 459 workers, 1.5 times the national share
- Theater square district (Arts, sport & recreation): 253 workers, 1.3 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.4% of valid votes in Szczecinek and Karol Nawrocki 43.6%. Turnout was 67.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 9.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Szczecinek, ul. 1 Maja (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
