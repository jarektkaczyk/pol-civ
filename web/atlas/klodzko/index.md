## City type and districts

City type: Tourist town. Hotels and restaurants: 2.6 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat kłodzki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 1,943 workers, 2.6 times the national share
- Theater square district (Arts, sport & recreation): 582 workers, 1.5 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 57.7% of valid votes in Kłodzko and Karol Nawrocki 42.3%. Turnout was 67.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 19.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kłodzko, ul. Szkolna (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
