Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/plock/

Last updated: 2026-11-28

The average gross wage in Płock was 10,718 zł a month in May 2026, the 5th highest for any of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Płock is a city with powiat rights in Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 52.0% of valid votes in Płock and Karol Nawrocki 48.0%. Turnout was 72.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Płock, ul. Reja (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
