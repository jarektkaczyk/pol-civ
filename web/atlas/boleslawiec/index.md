# Bolesławiec

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/boleslawiec/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Bolesławiec has the 10th cleanest air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 11.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Bolesławiec is a town in powiat bolesławiecki, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

These figures describe the jobs in powiat bolesławiecki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Government plaza district (Public administration & defence): 7,536 workers, 3.2 times the national share
- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 9,174 workers, 1.4 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 58.2% of valid votes in Bolesławiec and Karol Nawrocki 41.8%. Turnout was 68.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 11.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Osieczów (10 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
