# Poland in numbers

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Poland had 37,242,958 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 the population fell by 2.9%.

The country has 16 voivodeships, 2,479 gminas, 380 powiats and 1,026 towns.

Its GDP was 3.92 trillion zł in 2025. Mazowieckie, around Warszawa, is the largest voivodeship by population and produced 24.0% of the national GDP in 2024.

Each voivodeship and each town of 20,000 or more people has its own page below, with its figures and their sources.
