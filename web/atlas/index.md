# Poland in numbers

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Poland had 37,332,510 residents on 31 December 2025, 2.9% fewer than in 2015.

The country has 16 voivodeships, 2,479 gminas, 380 powiats and 1,026 towns.

Its GDP was 3.92 trillion zł in 2025. Mazowieckie, around Warszawa, is the largest voivodeship by population and produced 23.9% of the national GDP in 2024.

Each voivodeship and each town of 20,000 or more people has its own page below, with its figures and their sources.
