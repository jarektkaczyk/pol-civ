# Toruń

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/torun/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Toruń has 168 university students per 1,000 residents, the 7th highest share of the 84 towns of 20,000+ with a university.

Toruń is a city with powiat rights in Kujawsko-Pomorskie voivodeship. It is the seat of the regional assembly (sejmik) of Kujawsko-Pomorskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 63.8% of valid votes in Toruń and Karol Nawrocki 36.2%. Turnout was 74.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
