## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 44.2% of valid votes in the gmina of Skawina and Karol Nawrocki 55.8%. Turnout was 73.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kraków, ul. Bujaka (15 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Skawina belongs to the Kraków metro area, 1,468,599 residents in 40 gminas. Its core town is Kraków.

- Uniwersytet Jagielloński w Krakowie: University, 33,369 students on 31 December 2025, 12 km away
- Historic Centre of Kraków: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1978, 12 km away
- Kalwaria Zebrzydowska: the Mannerist Architectural and Park Landscape Complex and Pilgrimage Park: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1999, 16 km away
- Wieliczka and Bochnia Royal Salt Mines: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1978, 17 km away
