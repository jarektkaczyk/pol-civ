2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 44.2% of valid votes in the gmina of Skawina and Karol Nawrocki 55.8%. Turnout was 73.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
