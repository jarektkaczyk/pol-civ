# Gniezno

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/gniezno/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/gniezno/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Gniezno had 61,664 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 10.5% of its residents.

Gniezno is a town in powiat gnieźnieński, Wielkopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 60.2% of valid votes in Gniezno and Karol Nawrocki 39.8%. Turnout was 70.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
