# Marki

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/marki/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Marki grew by 53.5% between 2015 and 2025, the fastest growth of the 204 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Marki is a town in powiat wołomiński, Mazowieckie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 52.7% of valid votes in Marki and Karol Nawrocki 47.3%. Turnout was 79.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.2 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Warszawa, ul. Kondratowicza (8 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
