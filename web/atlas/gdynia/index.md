Gdynia expects 5,917 zł per resident from its share of personal income tax in 2026, the 10th highest of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Gdynia is a city with powiat rights in Pomorskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Port city. Port of Gdynia.

Jobs by employer seat on 31 December 2025, against the share of each kind of work in Poland.

- Harbor district (Transport & storage): 17,195 workers, 2.5 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 3,255 workers, 1.3 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 69.9% of valid votes in Gdynia and Karol Nawrocki 30.1%. Turnout was 78.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Gdynia, ul. Porębskiego (7 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Gdynia belongs to the Trójmiasto metro area (PL006F, PL053F), 1,133,819 residents in 19 gminas. Its core town is Gdańsk.

- Gdynia Modernist City Centre: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2026, in the town
- Port of Gdańsk: Busiest seaport by cargo tonnage: 75,324 thousand tonnes (2025), 17 km away
