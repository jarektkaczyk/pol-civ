Sopot

Sopot, Pomorskie

Sopot lost 16.2% of its residents between 2015 and 2025, the steepest fall of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Sopot is a city with powiat rights in Pomorskie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 72.5% of valid votes in Sopot and Karol Nawrocki 27.5%. Turnout was 80.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 13.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Sopot, ul. Bitwy Pod Płowcami (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
