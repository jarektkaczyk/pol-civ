# Świdnica

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/swidnica/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/swidnica/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Świdnica had 51,073 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 12.0% of its residents.

Świdnica is a town in powiat świdnicki, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 60.8% of valid votes in Świdnica and Karol Nawrocki 39.2%. Turnout was 70.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 14.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Wałbrzych, ul. Wysockiego (19 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
