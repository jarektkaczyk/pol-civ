# Piła

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/pila/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Piła had 68,762 residents on 31 December 2025, 7.2% fewer than in 2015.

Piła is a town in powiat pilski, Wielkopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 63.9% of valid votes in Piła and Karol Nawrocki 36.1%. Turnout was 71.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
