# Oleśnica

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/olesnica/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/olesnica/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Oleśnica had 34,498 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 7.4% of its residents.

Oleśnica is a town in powiat oleśnicki, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.9 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat oleśnicki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 12,226 workers, 1.9 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 58.2% of valid votes in Oleśnica and Karol Nawrocki 41.8%. Turnout was 71.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 16.2 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Wrocław, ul. Na Grobli (23 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
