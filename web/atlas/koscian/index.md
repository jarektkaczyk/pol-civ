# Kościan

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/koscian/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/koscian/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Kościan had 22,888 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 3.3% of its residents.

Kościan is a town in powiat kościański, Wielkopolskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 63.0% of valid votes in Kościan and Karol Nawrocki 37.0%. Turnout was 73.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
