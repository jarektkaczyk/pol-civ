# Kołobrzeg

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/kolobrzeg/

Last updated: 2026-11-28

Kołobrzeg has the 6th oldest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 49.3 years.

Kołobrzeg is a town in powiat kołobrzeski, Zachodniopomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 63.9% of valid votes in Kołobrzeg and Karol Nawrocki 36.1%. Turnout was 75.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 12.0 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Kołobrzeg, ul. Żółkiewskiego (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
