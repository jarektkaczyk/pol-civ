# Jaworzno

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/jaworzno/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Jaworzno had 84,835 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 8.2% of its residents.

Jaworzno is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 53.0% of valid votes in Jaworzno and Karol Nawrocki 47.0%. Turnout was 70.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality
