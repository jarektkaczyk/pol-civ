# Zgierz

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/zgierz/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/zgierz/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Zgierz had 52,137 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 8.4% of its residents.

Zgierz is a town in powiat zgierski, Łódzkie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.5% of valid votes in Zgierz and Karol Nawrocki 43.5%. Turnout was 73.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 17.7 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Łódź, ul. Legionów (9 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
