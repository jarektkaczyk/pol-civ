Warszawa

Warszawa, Mazowieckie

Warszawa is the capital and the largest city of Poland, with 1,866,929 residents on 30 June 2026.

Warszawa is the capital of Poland and a city with powiat rights in Mazowieckie voivodeship. It is the capital of Mazowieckie.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 68.2% of valid votes in Warszawa and Karol Nawrocki 31.8%. Turnout was 83.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 15.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Warszawa, al. Niepodległości (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
