# Cieszyn

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/cieszyn/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Cieszyn had 32,074 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 8.7% of its residents.

Cieszyn is a town in powiat cieszyński, Śląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Tourist town. Hotels and restaurants: 2.3 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat cieszyński, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 15,707 workers, 1.4 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 2,863 workers, 2.3 times the national share
- Theater square district (Arts, sport & recreation): 1,115 workers, 1.7 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 60.3% of valid votes in Cieszyn and Karol Nawrocki 39.6%. Turnout was 70.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality
