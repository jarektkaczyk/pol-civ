## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 64.4% of valid votes in the gmina of Piaseczno and Karol Nawrocki 35.6%. Turnout was 83.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 11.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Konstancin-Jeziorna, ul. Żeromskiego (11 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
