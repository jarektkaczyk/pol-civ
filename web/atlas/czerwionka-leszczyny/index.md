# Czerwionka-Leszczyny

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/czerwionka-leszczyny/

Czerwionka-Leszczyny had 26,031 residents on 31 December 2025.

Czerwionka-Leszczyny is a town in powiat rybnicki, Śląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Coal town. KWK Knurów-Szczygłowice.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat rybnicki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 3,396 workers or more, 1.4 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 355 workers, 1.3 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 38.3% of valid votes in the gmina of Czerwionka-Leszczyny and Karol Nawrocki 61.7%. Turnout was 70.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
