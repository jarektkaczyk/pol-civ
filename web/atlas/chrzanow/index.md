## City type and districts

City type: Coal town. ZG Janina.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat chrzanowski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 7,780 workers, 1.4 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.4% of valid votes in the gmina of Chrzanów and Karol Nawrocki 43.6%. Turnout was 70.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 15.2 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Trzebinia, os. Związku Walki Młodych (6 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
