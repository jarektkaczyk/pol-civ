# Opole

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/opole/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/opole/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Opole had 124,260 residents on 30 June 2026.

Opole is a city with powiat rights in Opolskie voivodeship. It is the capital of Opolskie.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 67.9% of valid votes in Opole and Karol Nawrocki 32.1%. Turnout was 73.6%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 16.0 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Opole, ul. Koszyka (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
