## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 65.9% of valid votes in Pruszcz Gdański and Karol Nawrocki 34.1%. Turnout was 75.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 13.0 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Gdańsk, ul. Lawendowe Wzgórze (7 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Pruszcz Gdański belongs to the Trójmiasto metro area (PL006F, PL053F), 1,133,819 residents in 19 gminas. Its core town is Gdańsk.

- Port of Gdańsk: Busiest seaport by cargo tonnage: 75,324 thousand tonnes (2025), 14 km away
- Gdynia Modernist City Centre: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2026, 30 km away
