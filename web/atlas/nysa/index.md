## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.3% of valid votes in the gmina of Nysa and Karol Nawrocki 48.7%. Turnout was 66.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
