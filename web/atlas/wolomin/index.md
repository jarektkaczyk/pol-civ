## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 43.6% of valid votes in the gmina of Wołomin and Karol Nawrocki 56.4%. Turnout was 78.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
