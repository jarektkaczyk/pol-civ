## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 45.0% of valid votes in Mława and Karol Nawrocki 55.0%. Turnout was 69.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
