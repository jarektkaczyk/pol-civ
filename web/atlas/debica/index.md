# Dębica

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/debica/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Dębica had 42,223 residents on 31 December 2025, 9.0% fewer than in 2015.

Dębica is a town in powiat dębicki, Podkarpackie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 37.9% of valid votes in Dębica and Karol Nawrocki 62.1%. Turnout was 72.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
