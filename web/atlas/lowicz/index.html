Łowicz

Łowicz, powiat łowicki, Łódzkie

Łowicz had 25,141 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 12.5% of its residents.

Łowicz is a town in powiat łowicki, Łódzkie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 48.1% of valid votes in Łowicz and Karol Nawrocki 51.9%. Turnout was 72.7%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
