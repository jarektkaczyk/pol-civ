# Dzierżoniów

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/dzierzoniow/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/dzierzoniow/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Dzierżoniów has the 9th oldest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 48.9 years.

Dzierżoniów is a town in powiat dzierżoniowski, Dolnośląskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.7 times the national share of workers.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.2% of valid votes in Dzierżoniów and Karol Nawrocki 43.8%. Turnout was 67.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
