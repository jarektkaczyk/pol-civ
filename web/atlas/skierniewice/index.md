# Skierniewice

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/skierniewice/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Skierniewice has the 10th most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 20.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Skierniewice is a city with powiat rights in Łódzkie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.9% of valid votes in Skierniewice and Karol Nawrocki 48.1%. Turnout was 76.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 20.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Żyrardów, ul. Roosevelta (21 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
