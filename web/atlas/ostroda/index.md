# Ostróda

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/ostroda/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/ostroda/
Last updated: 2026-11-28

Ostróda had 30,612 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 8.2% of its residents.

Ostróda is a town in powiat ostródzki, Warmińsko-Mazurskie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 1.7 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat ostródzki, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 8,355 workers, 1.7 times the national share
- Entertainment complex district (Hotels & restaurants): 805 workers, 1.4 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 62.0% of valid votes in Ostróda and Karol Nawrocki 38.0%. Turnout was 68.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 12.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Ostróda, ul. Piłsudskiego (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
