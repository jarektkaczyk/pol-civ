# Ruda Śląska

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/ruda-slaska/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/ruda-slaska/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Ruda Śląska had 126,922 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 8.8% of its residents.

Ruda Śląska is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 50.5% of valid votes in Ruda Śląska and Karol Nawrocki 49.5%. Turnout was 68.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.3 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Katowice, ul. Kossutha (8 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Ruda Śląska belongs to the Górnośląsko-Zagłębiowska metro area (PL010F, PL055F, PL061F, PL070F), 2,013,732 residents in 39 gminas. Its core town is Katowice.

- Tarnowskie Góry Lead-Silver-Zinc Mine and its Underground Water Management System: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2017, 18 km away
