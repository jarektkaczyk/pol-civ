# Knurów

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/knurow/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Knurów had 34,791 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 9.9% of its residents.

Knurów is a town in powiat gliwicki, Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 48.0% of valid votes in Knurów and Karol Nawrocki 52.0%. Turnout was 71.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 19.4 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Gliwice, ul. Mewy (6 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
