# Biała Podlaska

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/biala-podlaska/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/biala-podlaska/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Biała Podlaska had 53,266 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.9% of its residents.

Biała Podlaska is a city with powiat rights in Lubelskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 41.3% of valid votes in Biała Podlaska and Karol Nawrocki 58.7%. Turnout was 71.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 16.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Biała Podlaska, ul. Orzechowa (1 km).
