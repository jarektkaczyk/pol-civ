Registered unemployment around Jasło (powiat jasielski) was 13.1% in October 2026, the 7th highest for any of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 39.4% of valid votes in Jasło and Karol Nawrocki 60.6%. Turnout was 68.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 13.8 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Jaslo, ul. Sikorskiego (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
