# Łaziska Górne

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/laziska-gorne/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Łaziska Górne had 20,789 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 7.1% of its residents.

Łaziska Górne is a town in powiat mikołowski, Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 50.3% of valid votes in Łaziska Górne and Karol Nawrocki 49.7%. Turnout was 72.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
