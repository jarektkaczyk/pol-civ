## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 59.3% of valid votes in Stargard and Karol Nawrocki 40.7%. Turnout was 71.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 11.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Szczecin, ul. Andrzejewskiego (24 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
