# Mazowieckie

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/mazowieckie/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/mazowieckie/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Mazowieckie is the most populous voivodeship, with 5,502,043 residents on 30 June 2026.

Its capital is Warszawa.

It has 114 towns; 26 of them have 20,000 or more residents.

## Heritage, parks and landmarks

- Historic Centre of Warsaw: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1980
- Kampinoski Park Narodowy: National park, 1,300,000 visits in 2024
- Uniwersytet Warszawski: University, 39,852 students on 31 December 2025
- Politechnika Warszawska: University, 20,953 students on 31 December 2025
- Varso Tower: Tallest building in Poland: 310 m (2022)
- Warszawa-Chopin: Busiest airport by passengers: 24,079,757 passengers (2025)
