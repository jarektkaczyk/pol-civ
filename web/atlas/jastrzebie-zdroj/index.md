# Jastrzębie-Zdrój

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/jastrzebie-zdroj/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/jastrzebie-zdroj/
Last updated: 2026-11-27

Jastrzębie-Zdrój had 80,119 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 10.8% of its residents.

Jastrzębie-Zdrój is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 40.1% of valid votes in Jastrzębie-Zdrój and Karol Nawrocki 59.9%. Turnout was 69.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 19.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Jastrzębie-Zdrój, Al.J.Piłsudskiego/Harcerska (2 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
