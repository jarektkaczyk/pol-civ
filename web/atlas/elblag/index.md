# Elbląg

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/elblag/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Elbląg had 111,089 residents on 31 December 2025, 8.7% fewer than in 2015.

Elbląg is a city with powiat rights in Warmińsko-Mazurskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 59.6% of valid votes in Elbląg and Karol Nawrocki 40.4%. Turnout was 69.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 12.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Elbląg, ul. Bażyńskiego (3 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
