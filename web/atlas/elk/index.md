## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.3% of valid votes in Ełk and Karol Nawrocki 50.7%. Turnout was 61.9%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 12.5 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Ełk, ul. Piłsudskiego (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
