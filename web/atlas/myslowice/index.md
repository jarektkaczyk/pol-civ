# Mysłowice

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/myslowice/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Mysłowice had 69,864 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 6.3% of its residents.

Mysłowice is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 51.4% of valid votes in Mysłowice and Karol Nawrocki 48.6%. Turnout was 72.4%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.1 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Katowice, ul. Dudy-Gracza (10 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
