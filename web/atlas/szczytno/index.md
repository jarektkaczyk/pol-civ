# Szczytno

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/szczytno/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Registered unemployment around Szczytno (powiat szczycieński) was 11.5% in October 2026, the 10th highest for any of the 204 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Szczytno is a town in powiat szczycieński, Warmińsko-Mazurskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 52.6% of valid votes in Szczytno and Karol Nawrocki 47.4%. Turnout was 68.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
