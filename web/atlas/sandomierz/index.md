Sandomierz has the 3rd oldest population of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people: a median age of 49.9 years.

Sandomierz is a town in powiat sandomierski, Świętokrzyskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.8% of valid votes in Sandomierz and Karol Nawrocki 50.2%. Turnout was 68.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
