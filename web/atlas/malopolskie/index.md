# Małopolskie

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/malopolskie/
Polish version: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/pl/atlas/malopolskie/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Małopolskie has the most tourist nights per resident of the 16 voivodeships: 8.8 in 2025.

Its capital is Kraków.

It has 64 towns; 12 of them have 20,000 or more residents.

## Heritage, parks and landmarks

- Historic Centre of Kraków: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1978
- Wieliczka and Bochnia Royal Salt Mines: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1978
- Auschwitz Birkenau German Nazi Concentration and Extermination Camp (1940-1945): Memorial and museum, UNESCO World Heritage since 1979
- Kalwaria Zebrzydowska: the Mannerist Architectural and Park Landscape Complex and Pilgrimage Park: UNESCO World Heritage site since 1999
- Wooden Churches of Southern Małopolska: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2003
- Wooden Tserkvas of the Carpathian Region in Poland and Ukraine: UNESCO World Heritage site since 2013
- Tatrzański Park Narodowy: National park, 5,266,672 visits in 2025 (5,091,772 in 2024)
- Pieniński Park Narodowy: National park, 1,180,600 visits in 2024
- Uniwersytet Jagielloński w Krakowie: University, 33,369 students on 31 December 2025
