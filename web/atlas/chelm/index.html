Chełm

Chełm, Lubelskie

Chełm had 55,319 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 13.4% of its residents.

Chełm is a city with powiat rights in Lubelskie voivodeship.

2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 46.1% of valid votes in Chełm and Karol Nawrocki 53.9%. Turnout was 66.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.
