# Słupsk

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/slupsk/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Słupsk had 85,436 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 7.3% of its residents.

Słupsk is a city with powiat rights in Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 62.0% of valid votes in Słupsk and Karol Nawrocki 38.0%. Turnout was 70.0%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
