# Chorzów

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/chorzow/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Chorzów had 98,418 residents on 31 December 2025, 10.3% fewer than in 2015.

Chorzów is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 61.0% of valid votes in Chorzów and Karol Nawrocki 39.0%. Turnout was 67.5%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 18.3 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Katowice, ul. Kossutha (4 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.

## Metro area and places nearby

Chorzów belongs to the Górnośląsko-Zagłębiowska metro area (PL010F, PL055F, PL061F, PL070F), 2,021,558 residents in 39 gminas. Its core town is Katowice.
