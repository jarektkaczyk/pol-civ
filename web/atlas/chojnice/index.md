# Chojnice

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/chojnice/
Last updated: 2026-11-26

Chojnice had 37,906 residents on 30 June 2026. Between 2015 and 2025 it lost 4.8% of its residents.

Chojnice is a town in powiat chojnicki, Pomorskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.1% of valid votes in Chojnice and Karol Nawrocki 43.9%. Turnout was 69.2%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
