Częstochowa has the 3rd most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 24.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Częstochowa is a city with powiat rights in Śląskie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 56.4% of valid votes in Częstochowa and Karol Nawrocki 43.6%. Turnout was 72.8%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 24.9 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Częstochowa, ul. Zana (0 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
