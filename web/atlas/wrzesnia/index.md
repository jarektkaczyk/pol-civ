## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 53.7% of valid votes in the gmina of Września and Karol Nawrocki 46.3%. Turnout was 72.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Koalicja Obywatelska.
