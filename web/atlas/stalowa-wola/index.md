# Stalowa Wola

Source page: https://softonsofa.com/pol-civ/atlas/stalowa-wola/

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Stalowa Wola lost 14.9% of its residents between 2015 and 2025, the 4th steepest fall of the 203 Polish towns with 20,000 or more people.

Stalowa Wola is a town in powiat stalowowolski, Podkarpackie voivodeship.

## City type and districts

City type: Industrial town. Mines and factories: 2 times the national share of workers.

These figures describe the jobs in powiat stalowowolski, by employer seat, on 31 December 2025.

- Industrial zone district (Mining, Manufacturing): 13,308 workers, 2.0 times the national share

- Theater square district (Arts, sport & recreation): 658 workers, 1.6 times the national share

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 38.3% of valid votes in Stalowa Wola and Karol Nawrocki 61.7%. Turnout was 67.3%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 17.2 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Nisko, ul. Szklarniowa (7 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
