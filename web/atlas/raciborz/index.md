## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 53.2% of valid votes in Racibórz and Karol Nawrocki 46.8%. Turnout was 64.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 13.3 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Racibórz, Wojska Polskiego (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
