# Piotrków Trybunalski

Piotrków Trybunalski has the 5th most polluted air of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a measuring station within 25 km: PM2.5 averaged 21.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the nearest station.

Piotrków Trybunalski is a city with powiat rights in Łódzkie voivodeship.

## 2025 presidential election

In the presidential runoff of 1 June 2025, Rafał Trzaskowski won 49.7% of valid votes in Piotrków Trybunalski and Karol Nawrocki 50.3%. Turnout was 72.1%.

In the Sejm election of 15 October 2023 the largest committee there was Prawo i Sprawiedliwość.

## Air quality

PM2.5 averaged 21.6 µg/m³ in 2025 at the GIOŚ station Piotrków Trybunalski, ul. Krakowskie Przedmieście (1 km).

The median of the 154 towns of 20,000+ with a station within 25 km was 16.4 µg/m³.
